**VOICEOVER**:
> "Notice what happens when I ask a complex technical inquiry. Under the hood, our Agent Development Kit orchestrator decomposes the prompt, executes hybrid vector search across our indexed corporate corpus, generates a grounded synthesis using Gemini 3.8 Flash, and runs our Verification Critic.

If I click this citation pill, the Citation Drawer immediately slides in, revealing the exact matching quote, page number, and our 99.4% ground-truth confidence score — eliminating hallucinations completely."
